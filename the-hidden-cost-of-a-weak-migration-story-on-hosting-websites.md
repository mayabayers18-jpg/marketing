<h1 style="font-size: 32px; font-weight: 700; color: #111; margin-bottom: 16px;">The Hidden Cost of a Weak Migration Story on Hosting Websites</h1>

<img src="https://jpcdn.it/img/small/668691ac1e0442d88c98c82990a604f0.png" alt="Hosting Website Migration" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px; margin-bottom: 24px;">

<p style="margin-bottom: 18px;">Every hosting or cloud infrastructure company eventually runs into the same sales objection: prospects are already hosted somewhere else, and switching feels risky. Most hosting website templates never address this directly — they focus entirely on the new customer signing up fresh, and quietly ignore the much larger segment of buyers who are trying to leave a competitor.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 18px;">This is a real gap, because migration friction is often the actual reason a deal stalls, not price or features. A page that only says "get started free" is speaking to the wrong audience half the time.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 18px;">The fix is straightforward but rarely implemented well: a dedicated section addressing migration directly, ideally with credits, training resources, or hands-on technical support built into the offer. This single addition can shift a page from feeling like a beginner-only signup form to a serious infrastructure partner capable of handling complex transitions.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 18px;">Migration messaging works best when it's paired with the rest of a trustworthy structure — real product categories (apps, databases, storage, routing), uptime and data protection claims, and clear support signals in the navigation. Without that foundation, a migration CTA on its own won't do much.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 18px;">For anyone building or auditing a hosting company site, it's worth studying how one template handles this balance. A full walkthrough of a <a href="https://unicornplatform.com/blog/web-hosting-website-template/" style="color: #4f46e5; text-decoration: none; font-weight: 600;">hosting website template</a> covers exactly where the migration messaging succeeds and what else needs to be built around it — including the pricing gap that still needs solving.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 0;">If your hosting site doesn't speak to the buyer who's already hosted somewhere else, you're likely losing more deals than you realize.</p>
